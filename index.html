About Diabetic Retinopathy

What is Diabetic Retinopathy?

Diabetic retinopathy is a diabetes complication that affects eyes. It's caused by damage to the blood vessels of the light-sensitive tissue at the back of the eye (retina).

Stages of Diabetic Retinopathy

No DR: No detectable signs of retinopathy
Mild: Microaneurysms only
Moderate: More than just microaneurysms but less than severe NPDR
Severe: Any of the following: 20+ intraretinal hemorrhages, venous beading, IRMA
Proliferative DR: Neovascularization or vitreous/preretinal hemorrhage

Importance of Early Detection

Early detection and timely treatment can prevent up to 98% of severe vision loss from diabetic retinopathy. Regular screening is crucial for diabetic patients.
